# /stack and /kickoff: blueprints by level and comparisons that cite scores

/stack and /kickoff are the conversational front end of the stack map. `stack propose --level` lists the blueprints for a product's complexity with their computed scores, and `stack compare` says which scores separate two technologies. The skills present every recommendation with those scores, so a choice is explained, not asserted. The "read the docs first" rule now also covers layers agents know poorly in languages without a rule template, as a path-scoped generic rule.
