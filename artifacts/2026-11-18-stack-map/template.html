Every project is a stack of layers

The agent-os catalog: 24 layers in six groups, technologies with complexity and AI-friendliness, languages, providers with cost and EU regions, and reference stacks from a brochure site to a live streaming platform. A project's stack.yaml picks from it, and the agents use it to label work, choose tests, check the environment and recall lessons.
