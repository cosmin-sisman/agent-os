# Code of Conduct

agent-os follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable. That includes code, configuration or logs from someone's project pasted without their consent.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through [GitHub's private reporting form](https://github.com/cosmin-sisman/agent-os/security/advisories/new) (choose "Report a vulnerability" and mark it as a conduct report), or by contacting a maintainer on GitHub. Every report is reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow this code may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.
